ground truth

LineageBench — verified against the publishers' own records

Real Hub models whose parentage is documented by the publishing org, judged blind against all candidate parents by the actual verdict engine. Wrong-parent and cross-family comparisons serve as hard negatives.

Statistical evidence, not proof. Every relationship shown is a statement of statistical consistency between weight fingerprints — never an accusation. Merges are flagged but not attributed; distillation is invisible to weight forensics by construction; documented limitations (depth surgery, packed quantization) are shown as honest abstentions, not guesses.
